Concussion / Exertion Testing

Buffalo Concussion Treadmill Test

Run the BCTT, record each stage, calculate the exercise threshold, generate documentation, and export the results.

Clinical decision-support tool. Follow the official BCTT protocol and use clinical judgment for all stopping criteria and interpretation.

Baseline symptoms

Protocol Directions

  1. After 2 minutes seated, record resting HR, symptom severity, and RPE.
  2. Place the patient on the treadmill and obtain the Stage 0 standing HR before exercise.
  3. Begin at the selected brisk walking speed and 0% incline.
  4. At each 1-minute stage, record HR, RPE, symptoms, and observations.
  5. Increase incline by 1% each minute through 15%.
  6. After 15% incline, keep incline at 15% and increase speed by 0.4 mph each minute.
  7. Terminate for ≥3-point symptom exacerbation from baseline, voluntary exhaustion (RPE >17), patient request, or examiner/safety concern.
  8. If tolerated, cool down at 2.0 mph / 0% for 2 minutes and record recovery values.
